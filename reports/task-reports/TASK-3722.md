## Findings and fixes
**P1-1 — Horizontal page overflow at 861–1240px (including 1024px tablets) on every page.** TASK-3721 grew the header nav to 10 items; it no longer fit on one row (148px overflow at 1024px). Fix: below 1240px the logo stacks and nav items wrap (`AppHeader.astro`). Risk: taller header on tablets; no design-frozen hero asset touched.

**P1-2 — Offline miss on trailing-slash mismatch.** A page visited as `/principii/` was not found offline as `/principii` (header links use the slash-less form, search/sitemap the slashed one), contradicting "visited content works offline". Fix: `sw.js` falls back to the alternate-slash cache entry. Cache names/version unchanged, so existing field packs are not purged.

**P1-3 — Tampered localStorage could render a live `javascript:` link** in Spațiul meu (self-XSS class; no cross-user path and no import/share feature). Fix: `coach-state.ts` drops saved/favourite/recent refs whose `href` is not a same-origin path.

**P1-4 (repository integrity) — `git fsck --full` failed in `E:\ManualFC-clean`:** 4 corrupt loose objects (3 trees, 1 blob; mtime 2026-09-21, pre-existing) that also broke `git clone`. Origin was intact; the objects were restored byte-identically from a fresh bare clone of origin (corrupt files backed up outside the repo). History untouched; `fsck` now exits 0 and fresh clones work. Root cause of the local corruption is unknown.

Regression coverage: `tests/web/release-candidate.test.js` (10 real-browser tests; **6 fail against the pre-fix code**, verified by temporarily reverting the fixes). Reusable tooling: `scripts/audit_rc_browser.mjs`, `scripts/audit_rc_journey.mjs`.
